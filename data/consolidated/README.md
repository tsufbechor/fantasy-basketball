# Consolidated 8-category scoring diagnostics

Run from the project root with `./.venv/bin/python scripts/consolidate_scoring.py`. The script reads the Hashtag HTML snapshots, `data/career/player_mapping.csv`, `data/career/player_seasons.csv`, the ESPN 2026–27 roster snapshot, the ESPN league settings JSON, and the 2025–26 sleeper mention table. It writes only to `data/consolidated/`.

The current Hashtag projection page has 300 players, H2H averages, these eight selected categories: FG%, FT%, 3PM, PTS, REB, AST, STL, BLK. TO is unchecked. The page shows the 20% GP penalty and stores Yahoo as the position/ADP source. The page’s projected GP, original rank, and `TOTAL` are preserved as source fields; this calculation does not reproduce Hashtag’s hidden z-score formula or H2H floor.

## Outputs

- `tracked_players.csv`: one row for the union of 366 Hashtag IDs across the three pages, including the 10 without NBA career IDs. ESPN player IDs join by exact normalized Hashtag or reviewed NBA canonical name only. ESPN and NBA IDs are separate namespaces. Team labels are retained from both sources; known abbreviation variants are normalized only for the conflict flag.
- `hashtag_actuals.csv`: 732 tracked-player × season rows for 2024–25 and 2025–26. It combines what the saved Hashtag regular-season tables show (rank and rounded per-game values, if the player is within that page’s ranked rows) with NBA Stats regular-season totals, GP, rates, team(s), age, usage, and derived per-game counts. Missing table rows remain explicitly marked; they are not zero-filled.
- `hashtag_projections_2026_27.csv`: the 300 current projection rows plus exact source rank / `TOTAL` / GP penalty, team and position, rounded per-game stats, embedded FG/FT makes and attempts, qualitative positive/caution notes as JSON metadata, exact roster join, last-three-season age/usage/GP/MPG values, and deltas from 2025–26 actual per-game production. These notes and historical fields are not added to the score.
- `baseline_scoring.csv`: the same projection data plus the transparent per-game 8-category score and rank.
- `gp_sensitivity.csv`: one row per player for Hashtag GP and mechanical minus/plus-five-game scenarios. The calculation multiplies each raw per-game category contribution by scenario GP, then applies means and population SDs frozen from the baseline 300-player projected-total universe. It never multiplies a signed per-game z-score sum by GP. The +/-5 scenarios are stress tests, not probability estimates or learned GP adjustments.
- `espn_join_conflicts.csv`: exact-name joins that remain ambiguous. An empty file means none were encountered.
- `evidence_events.csv`: source news and ESPN injury-event metadata kept outside all numerical scores. Events join to Hashtag players by exact ESPN ID; unmatched events remain as rows. News publication, fetch, and first/last-seen timestamps are retained. Every row has `current_status=not_inferred`; ESPN reported injury status is kept separately, and rule-based news summaries are labeled `unverified_machine_summary`.
- `quality_report.json`: source hashes, selected page and league settings, row counts, mappings, duplicate rows, unmatched names, team conflicts, reference rates/scales, and integrity checks.

## Scoring and interpretation

For each of 3PM, PTS, REB, AST, STL, BLK, use the displayed per-game projection. Define FG impact as `derived FGM - p_ref_FG × FGA`, and FT impact similarly. The reference percentage is attempt-weighted over all 300 projected players: `p_ref_FG = Σ(FG% × FGA)/ΣFGA`, `p_ref_FT = Σ(FT% × FTA)/ΣFTA`. The HTML shows displayed percentages to three decimals and makes/attempts to one decimal, so those rounded values can disagree. The impact calculation therefore uses the displayed percentage times displayed attempts to derive makes; the separately displayed rounded makes are preserved for inspection.

For each of the eight per-game contributions, calculate a z-score against its mean and population standard deviation over the fixed 300-player projection universe. Add those eight z-scores for `our_raw8cat_zsum`. This is a raw sum with no H2H negative-category floor and no GP adjustment. `Hashtag TOTAL` remains a separate source field. All reported category means, scales, and ranks describe this chosen universe; changing the projection cutoff changes the diagnostic.

GP affects season totals and fantasy value through roster context. The current outputs provide raw totals under GP scenarios but do not claim a replacement-adjusted rank. A defensible replacement calculation needs the active waiver pool, fantasy lineup/roster limits, scoring-week schedules, and confirmed IR slot semantics. The league JSON is preserved in `quality_report.json`; its raw lineup slot IDs are not decoded into a replacement pool here. Evidence events and 2025–26 sleeper mentions are annotations only and do not affect any score. Sleeper mention counts/article labels are historical annotations joined by Hashtag ID only.

## Source and data caveats

The career mapping contains 366 Hashtag IDs, of which 356 map to NBA Stats player IDs (349 normalized full-name matches and seven manually reviewed aliases). The remaining 10 are unmatched future prospects. The 2024–25 Hashtag HTML is selected to the 2024–25 regular season and displays 200 unique players. The 2025–26 HTML is selected to the regular season and has 300 rendered rows but only 297 unique Hashtag IDs: Chaney Johnson appears four times with identical stats/rank-adjacent rows. The script collapses those duplicate rows after asserting their non-rank/non-ADP stats agree, retaining the smallest rank and reporting the duplicates.

The league export reports 12 teams and `H2H_CATEGORY`. ESPN roster matches are exact normalized names, not fuzzy guesses. A current roster absence means only that there was no exact name in the captured ESPN team roster file; it does not mean the player has no fantasy eligibility. Current Hashtag team and ESPN team are both retained. Qualitative Hashtag notes are editorial context, not modeled variables.

The evidence-event snapshot combines the five records in `research/news_pipeline/data/news.json` and the 101 rows in `research/rosters_injuries/injuries_2026-27.csv` (106 total). Joins use ESPN player IDs only; rows without a tracked Hashtag ID are retained. News summaries may be classifier-generated and are explicitly unverified; do not interpret their tags or text as confirmed medical status. Injury `reported_status`, source event date, and feed timestamp are copied as reported. `current_status` is deliberately `not_inferred` for every event, and none of this metadata changes projections or scores.
